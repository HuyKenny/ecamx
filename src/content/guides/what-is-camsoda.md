CamSoda is a free-to-watch live cam site founded in **2014** by **Daron Lundeen**. Like
Chaturbate and Stripchat, it runs on public rooms paid for by tips: you can watch without
an account, and viewers who want something buy tokens and spend them in the room.

rooms on the {{networks}} sites eCamx tracks.

## How CamSoda works

The room layout is familiar: stream, chat, a goal or tip menu, often a toy that reacts to
tips. Private shows are paid separately. If you have used any free cam site, nothing here
needs explaining.

What made CamSoda known was less the format than its stunts. It was one of the first sites
to stream in 360° VR, and over the years it ran headline-grabbing experiments — toys
linked to the price of Bitcoin, a "smell-o-vision" VR concept. Those are marketing, but
they say something about the site: it has always tried to look different from Chaturbate
rather than bigger.

One caution with that last number: viewer counts are reported by each site, and sites don't
necessarily count viewers the same way. The direction is clear; treat the exact gap with care.

## Is CamSoda safe?

CamSoda is an established, long-running business, and watching needs no account at all —
which is the safest way to try any cam site. The risks are the same as everywhere else:

- No CamSoda app exists in the App Store or Google Play (both stores ban pornographic
  apps), so don't install APKs that claim to be it.
- "CamSoda free tokens" generators are scams — read
  [why every token generator is a scam](/guides/free-tokens-scams/).
- Buy tokens only on the site itself, never through someone in chat.
