Stripchat is a live cam site built on the same idea as Chaturbate: public rooms anyone can
watch for free, paid for by viewers who tip in tokens. It launched on **3 May 2016**, is run
from Cyprus, and has grown into one of the largest adult sites on the web — Similarweb put
it at over 700 million visits a month in March 2025.

At our last check, {{live:stripchat}} Stripchat rooms were in the feed we receive.

## How Stripchat works

You open a room and watch. There is a chat next to the stream, a tip menu or goal, and
often an interactive toy that responds to tips. If you want more than the public show you
can pay for a private one. Tokens are bought in bundles with a card and spent in rooms;
models are paid out from what they earn.

That is close enough to Chaturbate that switching between the two takes no learning. The
differences are in **who** is on the site and **how** you can watch.

## VR cams

Stripchat's most distinctive feature is VR. Since 2019 a part of the site has been rooms
filmed with stereo cameras that you open in a VR headset's browser — it uses WebXR, so it
works on headsets such as Meta Quest and Apple Vision Pro without a separate app. On a
normal screen those rooms play as ordinary video.

That is also why "Stripchat VR" is searched more than 6,000 times a month in the US: no
other large free-to-watch site has made VR this central.

## Is there a Stripchat app?

Not in the official stores. Apple's guideline 1.1.4 rejects "overtly sexual or pornographic
material" and Google Play does not allow "apps that contain or promote sexual content…
including pornography". An app in either store using the Stripchat name is not the real
site, and APK files offered by download sites are not something to install — fake adult
apps are a common way to spread malware.

The website works in a phone browser, and you can add it to your home screen from the
