"Chaturbate app" and "Chaturbate mobile" are searched almost 4,000 times a month in the US
alone. The honest answer is shorter than most results make it look: **there is no real
Chaturbate app in the official stores, and you don't need one.**

## Why the App Store and Google Play don't have it

Both stores ban the very thing Chaturbate is. Apple's App Store Review Guidelines (rule
1.1.4) reject "overtly sexual or pornographic material", and Google Play's policy is just
as direct: "We don't allow apps that contain or promote sexual content or profanity,
including pornography."

That has two consequences:

1. An app in either store that calls itself Chaturbate cannot be streaming the real site's
   adult rooms. At best it is a thin wrapper or a dating app borrowing the name; at worst
   it is built to collect logins or sell subscriptions.
2. Anything that does offer the full site as an "app" has to come from outside the stores —
   which brings us to APKs.

## Why a Chaturbate APK is a bad idea

Search for the app on Android and you will find download sites offering an APK, sometimes
labelled "mod" or "premium". Installing one means switching off a protection on your phone
and running code nobody reviewed. Fake versions of popular adult apps are a known way to
spread malware, because the people searching for them are unlikely to report what
happened.

If a page asks you to install something to watch Chaturbate, close it. The website already
does everything an app would.

## The simple way: the browser

Chaturbate's website works in a phone browser. Open it in Safari on an iPhone or Chrome on Android
and rooms play like any other video. A few things make it nicer:

- **Add it to your home screen.** Safari: share button → *Add to Home Screen*. Chrome:
  menu → *Add to Home screen*. You get an icon that opens straight into the site, with
  nothing installed.
- **Tap to unmute.** Phones start videos muted; one tap on the stream turns the sound on.
- **Rotate for full screen.** Landscape gives you the full video; portrait keeps the chat
  under it.
- **Use Wi-Fi for long sessions.** An HD room uses roughly as much data as any HD video
  stream.

## If Chaturbate won't load on your phone

Most "Chaturbate not working on mobile" problems fall into three groups:

- **Age verification.** In some US states and countries the site asks you to verify your
  age first. That screen is part of the site, not an error.
- **Blocked by the network.** Office, school and some mobile networks filter adult sites.
  Try another connection.
- **The site is actually down.** Rare, but it happens. Our
  [Chaturbate status page](/status/chaturbate/) shows what our own check saw a few minutes
  ago.
