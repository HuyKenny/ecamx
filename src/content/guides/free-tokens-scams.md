"Chaturbate token hack no survey", "tokens generator exe", "free Stripchat tokens" — these
are searched well over a thousand times a month in the US. Every one of those searches ends
at the same kind of page, and none of them works. Here is why, so you don't have to find
out the expensive way.

## Why a token generator cannot exist

Tokens are not a file on your computer or a number inside an app. They are a **balance in
your account on the site's servers**, created when a payment goes through. When you tip,
your browser asks the site to move tokens from your balance to the performer's, and the
site checks the balance before it does.

A program on your device has no way into that balance. To "generate" tokens it would have
to break into the site's payment system — and if someone could do that, they would not be
giving it away on a download page with a countdown timer.

## What the "generators" really do

They make money from you in one of three ways:

1. **They steal your login.** The page asks for your username and password "to send the
   tokens". It sends nothing; it now has your account, and with it any saved card or
   tokens you did buy.
2. **They sell your time.** "Human verification" or "complete one offer to unlock" means
   surveys, app installs or subscriptions that pay the scammer per completion. The tokens
   never arrive, because there are none.
3. **They install malware.** A file called something like "tokens generator.exe" or a
   "mod APK" runs on your device with your permissions. Adult-themed bait is popular
   precisely because victims rarely report it.

The "no survey" versions are not safer — they usually skip straight to option 1 or 3.

## Red flags in one list

- It asks for your **password**.
- It asks you to **download** anything — exe, APK, browser extension.
- It promises an amount of tokens and a **countdown** or "only 3 left".
- It needs "**human verification**" through surveys or offers.
- It is a YouTube video or comment with a link in the description.
- It is someone in a room's chat offering tokens cheaper than the site.

Any one of these is enough to close the page.

## What is genuinely free

More than people expect. On the free-to-watch sites — Chaturbate, Stripchat, CamSoda,
Cam4, BongaCams — public rooms cost nothing and need no account. You only pay to tip, for
private shows and for extras.

Sites do occasionally run their own promotions — Stripchat, for example, offered free
tokens to new users in 2020. Real promotions are announced on the site itself and never
ask for your password anywhere else.

If you do decide to buy tokens, know the real price first: on Chaturbate two independent
listings agree on $10.99 for 100 tokens and $79.99 for 1,000 — see
[what Chaturbate tokens cost](/site/chaturbate/tokens/).
