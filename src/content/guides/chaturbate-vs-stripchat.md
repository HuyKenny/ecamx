Chaturbate and Stripchat are the two giants of free-to-watch cams, and they work the same
way: public rooms anyone can open, paid for by viewers who tip in tokens. If you know one,
you can use the other in a minute. The differences are in **how big** each is, **who**
broadcasts there and **how** you can watch.

At our last rebuild {{live:chaturbate}} rooms were live on Chaturbate and {{live:stripchat}}
on Stripchat — {{share:chaturbate}} and {{share:stripchat}} of every live room across the
{{networks}} sites eCamx tracks.

## The short answer

| | Chaturbate | Stripchat |
|---|---|---|
| Launched | 26 February 2011 | 3 May 2016 |
| Run by | Multi Media LLC, Irvine, California | from Cyprus |
| Free to watch, no account | Yes | Yes |
| Paid with | Tokens | Tokens |
| VR rooms | No | Yes, since 2019 |
| Viewer counts in the feed we receive | Yes | No |
| App in App Store / Google Play | No | No |

Pick **Chaturbate** if you want the most choice at any hour and like seeing how busy a room is
before you open it. Pick **Stripchat** if you are looking for couples or want to watch in VR.

## Who broadcasts on each — measured

We compared every live room in both feeds on 18 November 2026 at 02:00 UTC:

| Broadcasters | Chaturbate | Stripchat |
|---|---|---|
| Women | 69% | 57% |
| Men | 17% | 17% |
| Couples | 4% | 17% |
| Trans | 10% | 9% |

Men and trans performers make up about the same share on both. The real difference is
**couples**: proportionally about four times as many on Stripchat. Chaturbate is still the
bigger site, so in absolute numbers the gap is smaller than the percentages suggest — but if
couples are what you came for, Stripchat is the better place to start.

## VR: the one real format difference

Stripchat added VR rooms in 2019. They are filmed with stereo cameras and open in a VR
headset's browser through WebXR, so they work on headsets such as Meta Quest without a
separate app. On a normal screen they play as ordinary video. Chaturbate has nothing
comparable; its rooms are standard video. Our guide to
[VR cams vs regular cams](/guides/vr-cams-vs-regular-cams/) explains what you need.

## How busy the rooms are

Chaturbate reports how many people are watching each room, so we can measure it: on our
[top Chaturbate models](/site/chaturbate/top/) page every name is ranked by the biggest
audience we recorded ourselves. Stripchat does not send viewer counts in the feed we
receive, so we cannot say how big its rooms are — and any list that ranks Stripchat rooms
"by popularity" against Chaturbate's is guessing on that point.

## What tokens cost

On Chaturbate, two independent price listings agree that 100 tokens cost $10.99 and 1,000
cost $79.99 — see the [Chaturbate token calculator](/site/chaturbate/tokens/) for every
bundle and when we last checked. Stripchat does not show prices to visitors who are not
signed in, and we have not been able to confirm its bundles from two independent sources, so
we do not print a number for it. Neither site needs tokens just to watch.

## Apps and safety

Neither site is in the App Store or Google Play: Apple's guideline 1.1.4 rejects "overtly
sexual or pornographic material", and Google Play bans sexual content. Both work in a phone
browser. An app in either store using these names is not the real site, and "token
generators" for either one are scams — see [free tokens scams](/guides/free-tokens-scams/).
