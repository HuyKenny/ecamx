Chaturbate is a live webcam site where adults broadcast from home and anyone over 18 can
watch. It is the biggest site we track: at our last check {{live:chaturbate}} rooms were
live on it, which is {{share:chaturbate}} of every live room across the {{networks}} cam
sites eCamx follows.

The short version: **watching is free, tipping is not**. Everything else about the site
follows from that one design choice.

## How a Chaturbate room works

Every broadcaster has a public room. You open it and see the stream and a chat beside it —
no account needed to watch. What happens on camera is driven by tips. Most rooms show a
**goal** ("500 tokens left until…") or a **tip menu** with fixed prices for requests, and
many broadcasters connect an interactive toy that reacts to each tip.

So the audience pays together for what everyone sees. That is the opposite of the older
model used by sites such as Streamate, where the free part is a preview and the real show
is a paid one-to-one session billed per minute.

A broadcaster can also take a room **private**: one viewer pays per minute and everyone
else sees a "private show in progress" screen. When we measured on 18 November 2026,
434 of 7,209 live Chaturbate rooms — about 6% — were in a private or hidden show at that
moment. The other 94% were open to anyone.

## What is free and what you pay for

| Free, no account | Needs tokens |
|---|---|
| Watching any public room | Tipping and tip-menu requests |
| Reading chat | Private and spy shows |
| Browsing categories and tags | Some fan-club and photo extras |
| Following broadcasters (free account) | Chatting in rooms that limit chat to tippers |

Tokens are bought in bundles. Two independent price listings agree that 100 tokens cost
$10.99 and 1,000 tokens cost $79.99, so one token is worth roughly $0.08–$0.11 to a
viewer depending on the bundle. Chaturbate does not publish prices to anyone who is not
signed in; [our token page](/site/chaturbate/tokens/) shows what we could verify and when.

On the other side, a broadcaster is credited **$0.05 per token** and needs to earn at least
$50 before Chaturbate pays out. The difference between what viewers pay and what models
receive is the site's cut.

## What the rooms actually look like

The front page shows the biggest rooms, which gives a skewed picture. Our snapshot of all
7,209 live Chaturbate rooms on 18 November 2026 at 02:00 UTC looked like this:

| Viewers in the room | Share of live rooms that report viewers |
|---|---|

| 10–99 | 44% |

The biggest room had 30,484 viewers, and the top 1% of rooms held 72% of everyone
watching. In other words, Chaturbate is a handful of very large shows on top of thousands
of small rooms where the chat is a real conversation.

The same snapshot split the broadcasters as 69% women, 17% men, 10% trans and 4% couples.
That mix is why Chaturbate has large [men](/guys/), [trans](/trans/) and
[couples](/couples/) sections, not just one category of performer.

## Is Chaturbate safe and legit?

Chaturbate has run since **26 February 2011** and is owned by **Multi Media LLC** of Irvine,
California. It is one of the most visited websites in the world — Similarweb placed it 40th
worldwide in April 2025 — and it operates as a normal, registered business.

It has also had to adjust to new laws. In March 2024 the Texas attorney general sued the
site over the state's age-verification law; in April 2024 Chaturbate settled, paid
$675,000 and added age verification. Depending on where you live, you may be asked to
verify your age before you can watch.

The practical risks are mostly outside the site:

- **Fake apps.** Apple and Google do not allow pornographic apps in their stores, so an
  "official Chaturbate app" there cannot be the real site. See
  [our guide to Chaturbate on mobile](/guides/chaturbate-app/).
- **Token generators.** Anything that promises free tokens is a scam or malware — tokens
  live on Chaturbate's servers and cannot be generated on your device. More in
  [free tokens: why every generator is a scam](/guides/free-tokens-scams/).
- **Look-alike domains.** Typos such as "chaterbate" or "chaturbate.cim" are searched
  thousands of times a month. Type the address carefully or use a bookmark.
