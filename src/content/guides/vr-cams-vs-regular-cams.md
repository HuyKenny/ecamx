"VR cams" sounds like a different product from a normal live cam. In practice it is the
same idea, performers streaming live and viewers tipping, with a different camera on one
side and a headset on the other. Whether that is worth the effort depends on what you
want from a session. This guide sticks to what the sites and their documentation say.

## What a VR cam room is

A regular room is a flat video in a browser window with a chat next to it. A VR room is
filmed with a stereo camera, usually a 180° view, so each eye gets a slightly different
picture. Put a headset on and the room has depth, and you can look around inside the
frame.

Two sites are the usual answer to "which ones have it". Stripchat added VR rooms in 2019
and it works through WebXR, the browser standard for VR, on headsets such as Meta Quest
and Apple Vision Pro, with no separate app. CamSoda was one of the first sites to stream
in 360° VR. Most other big free sites have not made VR a feature. See our guides to
[Stripchat](/guides/what-is-stripchat/) and [CamSoda](/guides/what-is-camsoda/).

## What you need

- **A headset with a browser.** A standalone headset that can open websites is enough;
  WebXR means the site itself is the app.
- **A solid connection.** Stereo video carries two views, so it is heavier than a flat
  stream of the same sharpness. Wi-Fi close to the router beats a weak signal.
- **Patience for setup.** Putting a headset on, finding the room and entering VR mode
  takes longer than clicking a thumbnail.

A regular room needs a browser and nothing else. That is why almost all cam viewing,
including ours, happens on flat screens.

## Side by side

| | VR cam | Regular cam |
|---|---|---|
| Hardware | Headset with a browser | Any phone, tablet or computer |
| Picture | 3D, 180° or 360° view | Flat video |
| Choice of rooms | A small part of each site | The whole roster |
| Chat and tipping | Works differently per site and device | Chat next to the video |
| Session length | Headsets get tiring | As long as you like |
| Cost to watch | Public rooms free, tips paid | Public rooms free, tips paid |

We do not publish a count of live VR rooms: our feeds do not flag them reliably, and a
number we cannot back up does not belong in a guide. What we do know is that regular
rooms make up almost everything on the sites we track. At our last check,
{{live:chaturbate}} Chaturbate rooms and {{live:stripchat}} Stripchat rooms were live in
the feeds we receive, and that is the pool a flat-screen viewer chooses from.

## What VR does better

Depth is real. Distance, size and eye contact feel different when the video fills your
field of view instead of sitting in a window. For viewers who want to feel in the room,
that is the point, and nothing on a flat screen reproduces it.

## What regular rooms do better

- **Choice.** You can scan dozens of rooms in a minute and move on.
- **Interaction.** Chat, tip menus and goals are designed for a flat page.
- **Convenience.** No charging, no straps, no motion discomfort.
- **Comparison.** On eCamx you can [see which rooms are live on every site](/) and filter by
  category, which is much harder inside a headset.

## Pre-recorded VR is a separate thing

Do not mix up live VR cams with recorded VR videos. A recorded scene is shot once, in
high resolution, and plays on demand with no chat. If depth is what you are after and
live interaction is not, a recorded library is the easier route; DudeVR keeps a
[catalogue of 4K VR videos](https://dudevr.com/category/4k-vr-porn/) you can browse
before you own a headset.

## Which should you pick

Pick VR if you already own a headset, you care about the sense of presence, and you are
fine with fewer rooms. Pick regular cams for variety, for the phone in your pocket, or
for a short look at what is live. Many viewers use both: flat rooms for browsing, VR for
the occasional longer session.
