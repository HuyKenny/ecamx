A **cam girl** is a woman who broadcasts live video of herself on a webcam site and earns
money from the people watching. "Cam model" is the broader word for the same job — it
covers men, couples and trans performers as well — and **camming** is the work itself.

It is a bigger world than most people think. At our last check {{live:all}} rooms were
live at the same moment across the {{networks}} cam sites eCamx follows.

## What a cam show actually is

A cam show is not a recorded video. It is a live room: a stream from the performer's
camera and a chat next to it, where viewers type and the performer answers out loud. Most
of what happens is conversation, with explicit moments driven by tips.

On the big free sites (Chaturbate, Stripchat, CamSoda) a room usually has:

- **A tip menu** — fixed prices for specific requests.
- **A goal** — a token target ("1,000 tokens until…") that the whole room chips in for.
- **An interactive toy** — a device that vibrates when someone tips, by an amount tied to
  the tip. It turns tipping into part of the show.
- **Private shows** — one viewer pays per minute and gets the performer to themselves.

Viewers pay with **tokens**, a site currency bought in bundles. On Chaturbate, for
example, two independent price listings agree that 100 tokens cost $10.99.

## Who cams — in our numbers

On 18 November 2026 at 02:00 UTC we looked at all 14,330 rooms live across the sites we
track. 72% were women. The rest were men, trans performers and couples — and the mix
depends a lot on the site: couples are about one in six rooms on Stripchat but one in
twenty-five on Chaturbate.

Audiences are just as uneven. On Chaturbate, the largest site:

- half of all live rooms had **fewer than 10 viewers**,
- one room in twenty had 100 or more,
- just 34 rooms had more than 1,000,
- and the top 1% of rooms held **72% of everyone watching**.

So "cam girl" covers both: a handful of performers with audiences the size of a small
concert, and thousands of rooms where a viewer is talking to someone almost one-on-one.

## Free rooms vs paid shows

There are two business models, and they feel very different:

1. **Free-to-watch, tip-funded** (Chaturbate, Stripchat, CamSoda, Cam4, BongaCams). The
   show is public and funded by the room. You can watch without an account.
2. **Pay-per-minute** (Streamate, XLoveCam and similar). The free part is a preview; the
   real show is a private session billed by the minute.

eCamx shows both kinds side by side, and we only call a site free where watching really is.

## Is it safe and legal?

For viewers, watching a public room on an established site is legal for adults in most
countries, and you can do it without an account. Some places now require age verification
before you can watch, and sites have added it.
